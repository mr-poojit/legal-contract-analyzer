MASTER SERVICES AGREEMENT (2024 REVISED EDITION)

This Master Services Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into on March 1, 2024 ("Effective Date"), by and between Apex Global Technologies Inc., a Delaware corporation ("Client"), and Vertex Cloud Solutions LLC, a California limited liability company ("Vendor").

ARTICLE 1: SERVICES AND DELIVERABLES
1.1 Scope of Services. Vendor shall perform the professional software development and technical consulting services ("Services") as specified in each Statement of Work ("SOW") executed hereunder.
1.2 Deliverables. Vendor warrants that all Deliverables will conform in all material respects with the applicable specifications for an extended period of ninety (90) days from delivery.
1.3 Acceptance. Client shall have fifteen (15) business days from delivery of any Deliverable to inspect and test the Deliverable. If Client fails to notify Vendor of rejection within such period, the Deliverable shall be deemed accepted.

ARTICLE 2: FEES AND PAYMENT TERMS
2.1 Fees. Client shall pay Vendor the fees set forth in each applicable SOW.
2.2 Invoicing. Vendor shall submit invoices bi-weekly. All invoices are payable net forty-five (45) days from the date of invoice.
2.3 Late Payments. Late payments shall accrue interest at a rate of 1.0% per month or the highest rate permitted by California law, whichever is less.
2.4 Taxes. All fees are exclusive of applicable federal, state, or local sales, use, or value-added taxes, which shall be the sole responsibility of Client.

ARTICLE 3: CONFIDENTIALITY, DATA SECURITY, AND ARTIFICIAL INTELLIGENCE
3.1 Confidential Information. Each party agrees to hold in confidence all proprietary and non-public technical, business, and financial information disclosed by the other party.
3.2 Standard of Care. Each party shall use at least the same degree of care it uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than reasonable care.
3.3 Exclusions. Confidential Information shall not include information that: (a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was already known to the receiving party; or (c) is independently developed without reference to the disclosing party's information.
3.4 Data Security and SOC 2 Compliance. Vendor shall maintain SOC 2 Type II certification throughout the term and implement multi-factor authentication, end-to-end AES-256 encryption at rest and in transit, and continuous threat monitoring for all Client Data.
3.5 AI Training Restrictions. Vendor explicitly covenants that no Client Data, confidential information, or custom code shall be inputted into, ingested by, or used to train or fine-tune any public, private, or third-party Artificial Intelligence models or Large Language Models without prior written approval from Client.

ARTICLE 4: INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY RIGHTS
4.1 Client Materials. Client retains all right, title, and interest in and to all materials, data, and intellectual property provided by Client to Vendor.
4.2 Work Product. Subject to full payment of all fees, Vendor hereby assigns to Client all right, title, and interest in and to custom code and deliverables created exclusively for Client under an SOW.
4.3 Pre-Existing IP. Vendor retains ownership of all pre-existing software, frameworks, libraries, and tools ("Vendor Tools"). Vendor grants Client an irrevocable, non-exclusive, perpetual, worldwide, royalty-free license to use Vendor Tools incorporated into Deliverables.

ARTICLE 5: WARRANTIES AND DISCLAIMERS
5.1 Performance Warranty. Vendor warrants that Services will be performed in a professional, workmanlike manner in accordance with prevailing industry standards.
5.2 Security Warranty. Vendor warrants that Deliverables shall contain no viruses, malware, backdoors, or malicious code designed to disrupt Client operations.
5.3 Disclaimer. EXCEPT AS EXPRESSLY PROVIDED HEREIN, NEITHER PARTY MAKES ANY WARRANTIES, EXPRESS OR IMPLIED, INCLUDING WITHOUT LIMITATION ANY IMPLIED WARRANTIES OF MERCHANTABILITY OR FITNESS FOR A PARTICULAR PURPOSE.

ARTICLE 6: INDEMNIFICATION
6.1 Vendor Indemnification. Vendor shall defend, indemnify, and hold harmless Client, its officers, directors, and employees from and against any third-party claims alleging that the Deliverables infringe any worldwide patent, copyright, trademark, or trade secret, or arising from Vendor's gross negligence or willful misconduct.
6.2 Client Indemnification. Client shall defend and indemnify Vendor from any third-party claims arising from Client Materials or unauthorized modifications to Deliverables.
6.3 Procedure. The indemnified party must give prompt written notice of any claim and allow the indemnifying party sole control of defense and settlement.

ARTICLE 7: LIMITATION OF LIABILITY
7.1 Cap on Liability. EXCEPT FOR (A) BREACHES OF SECTION 3 (CONFIDENTIALITY AND AI RESTRICTIONS), (B) INDEMNIFICATION OBLIGATIONS UNDER SECTION 6, OR (C) DATA PRIVACY BREACHES, NEITHER PARTY'S TOTAL AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL EXCEED TWO TIMES (2X) THE TOTAL FEES PAID OR PAYABLE BY CLIENT UNDER THE APPLICABLE SOW IN THE TWELVE (12) MONTHS PRECEDING THE CLAIM.
7.2 Super-Cap for Data Breaches. FOR DATA SECURITY OR PRIVACY BREACHES UNDER SECTION 3, VENDOR'S MAXIMUM AGGREGATE LIABILITY SHALL BE CAPPED AT $5,000,000 USD.
7.3 Consequential Damages Waiver. IN NO EVENT SHALL EITHER PARTY BE LIABLE FOR ANY INDIRECT, INCIDENTAL, SPECIAL, OR CONSEQUENTIAL DAMAGES, INCLUDING LOSS OF PROFITS, DATA, OR BUSINESS INTERRUPTION.

ARTICLE 8: NON-SOLICITATION (NON-COMPETE REMOVED)
8.1 Non-Solicitation. During the term of this Agreement and for one (1) year thereafter, neither party shall directly solicit for employment any employee of the other party involved in the performance of Services.
8.2 Independent Contractors. The parties explicitly agree that previous non-competition restrictions have been removed to comply with California Business and Professions Code Section 16600.

ARTICLE 9: TERM AND TERMINATION
9.1 Term. This Agreement commences on the Effective Date and continues for a period of three (3) years unless terminated earlier in accordance with this Section.
9.2 Termination for Convenience. Either party may terminate this Agreement without cause upon thirty (30) days' prior written notice to the other party.
9.3 Termination for Cause. Either party may terminate immediately if the other party breaches any material term and fails to cure such breach within fifteen (15) days of written notice.

ARTICLE 10: GENERAL PROVISIONS
10.1 Governing Law. This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of Delaware, without regard to conflict of laws principles.
10.2 Dispute Resolution. Any dispute arising out of this Agreement shall be resolved through binding arbitration administered by JAMS in Wilmington, Delaware.
10.3 Entire Agreement. This Agreement constitutes the complete agreement between the parties and supersedes all prior agreements, representations, and understandings.
